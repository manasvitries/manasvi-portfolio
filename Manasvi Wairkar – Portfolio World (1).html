How to play

  • Walk with W A S D, look with the mouse, and press E next to the red car to get in.
  • Drive: W gas, S brake, A / D steer.
  • Press Enter to zoom the nearest billboard. Press Enter again to close it.
  • Esc frees the mouse. H or the ? button opens this panel.
  • Keys 1–6 or the bottom bar show that billboard on screen. Press the same key again, Enter or Esc to close it. Space jumps on foot. E also gets you out of the car when it’s slow.
  • F honks, L toggles headlights, N switches day and night, M plays music, C toggles cruise control.
  • On phones, use the on-screen buttons (🚗 get in, 🔍 zoom).
  • 🎁 Surprise: drive the car off the cliff at the end of the road!
